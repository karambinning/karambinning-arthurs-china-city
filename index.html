What we cook

No. 01

Sweet & sour

Pork balls, chicken balls, king prawn — battered, tossed, with that bright red house sauce on the side. The thing High Wycombe’s been ordering on Friday nights since forever.

No. 02

Chow mein & noodles

Chicken, beef, prawn, mixed, special. Soft noodles, the right amount of beansprout, a fistful of spring onion. Tossed hard, served fast.

No. 03

Rice & fried rice

Boiled, egg fried, special fried, singapore. The egg fried gets ordered with almost everything that leaves the building.

No. 04

Curry & black bean

House curry sauce, satay, black bean, oyster sauce. Saucy, generous, the sort of thing you want against a cold High Wycombe evening.

No. 05

Salt & pepper

Chips, ribs, chicken, squid. Fried hot, hit with chilli, onion and pepper from the wok. Crackle & heat in the bottom of a foil tray.

No. 06

Crispy duck

Quarter, half, or whole. Pancakes, hoisin, cucumber, spring onion. The dish you order for the table, eaten standing in the kitchen.
